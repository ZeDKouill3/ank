Measured on CI, run 39076435984 (main, merge of #645) and run 39076662788 (PR #646): both
red on windows-latest only, both on `concurrent_readers_of_a_cold_corpus_of_this_size_all_answer`,
and neither on its assertion. Each panicked at round 7 or 8 of 20, every round before it
reporting 0 refused of 8, at the `std::fs::copy(..).unwrap()` of `copy_tree`, with
`Os { code: 2, kind: NotFound }`.

The fixture copies the live `.ank/` of the workspace, index included. Other tests of the
suite read that index at the same time, and SQLite deletes `-wal` and `-shm` when the last
connection closes: `read_dir` lists the file, and it is gone by the `copy`. The copy is
pointless anyway, since `index_file::remove_all` deletes every `index.db*` on the next line.
Twenty rounds under CI (775e084b) made twenty copies spread over 85 seconds of suite, which
is why it began failing now, and Windows is the platform slow enough to lose it.

The fix removes the cause and adds no retry: the fixture never reads what it is about to delete.
