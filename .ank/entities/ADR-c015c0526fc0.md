PR #621 (ZeDKouille) made screen 3 the blocked_by graph and drew it in the region ADR-ac6be1ebe9aa reserved for a detail panel that shows the selected row. The maintainer reviewed the design on 2026-10-17, kept it, and merged it as 7c9efe1c. Three sentences of ADR-ac6be1ebe9aa no longer describe the reader: the detail panel showing the selected row, a digit naming every panel, and every panel title pressing its digit.

**Why an amendment and not a supersession.** ADR-9ee76b578257 says a decision that changes in part is amended, and the rest stays binding. Everything else in ADR-ac6be1ebe9aa still holds: the claims panel, the key line, keystroke input, the confirmation before any write, colour that only repeats a character, the ASCII fallback, and ADR-8bd76e8d7c4e untouched. A supersession would copy about 4000 characters to change three sentences, and would make some two hundred citations across crates/ank-tui stale until rewritten (ADR-3b6ba766a42e).

**What the old clause protected is kept.** The detail never followed the cursor, so moving it spawned no show that could renew the reader's own claim (ADR-0bb7ea8991bc). The graph is read through ank graph --json, which renews nothing and coordinates nothing, and show still waits for a person to open a row.
