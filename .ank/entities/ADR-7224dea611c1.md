## Context

ADR-d8a328e311b9 and ADR-a2faf59f6ce0 were ratified on 2026-10-11, and
ADR-c621cb9c6739 on 2026-10-13. Measured on 2026-10-14 (c859d01f): no code
reads `env`, `inputs`, `image` or `reuse_max_age` (zero hits over `crates/`),
`ank help` lists no `verify`, and five of the twelve open tasks serve the three
decisions. The audit that found this is in the log of ADR-d8a328e311b9.

Read together, the three decide two different things. One is cheap and
answers a measured need: a verifier declares which files it reads, `ank verify`
runs the verifiers a change touches, and `done` does not rerun what passed on
the same bytes a moment ago. The other is an execution environment for
verifiers, an OCI image pinned by digest with a platform and a network policy,
whose only consumer is "an independent executor" that does not exist, that the
decision itself says the binary never runs, and that nothing in this
repository plans to build. ADR-d8a328e311b9 rejected containers in ank as "a
runtime dependency to a tool with nothing to run"; the `env` block is the
declaration half of that runtime, kept against the day somebody writes the
other half.

The maintainer decided on 2026-10-14, with the thesis of ADR-9a0076dd0896 in
view: keep `inputs`, keep `verify`, keep local reuse, and withdraw `env` until
an executor exists to honour it.

## What is amended, clause by clause

From ADR-d8a328e311b9, withdrawn: the `env` block (image, platform, vars,
network), hermeticity as a derived property, and the executor paragraphs.
Kept: `inputs` globs, the definition hash covering them, and the sentence that
without `inputs` the whole tree is the input.

From ADR-a2faf59f6ce0, withdrawn: reuse of "a passing attestation with the
same key from an executor honouring env for a hermetic verifier". Kept: the
content key, the local registry under the git common directory, what `done`
may reuse from it and for how long, the `reused` and `by` fields, and
`--fresh`.

ADR-c621cb9c6739 is untouched: `ank verify` selects by `inputs` and feeds the
same registry.

## Consequences

- TASK-dc04757dfecb was claimed, and its criterion frozen, while this was
  being written, and its holder finished it on 2026-10-14 (46fa39c7) with
  the `env` keys parsed, validated and hashed. That close stands as proved.
  TASK-91cefb36af81 takes `env` out again, and runs only once this is accepted:
  until then ADR-d8a328e311b9 still binds.
- TASK-413bcf394090 gains the clause that a detached attestation is never
  reused.
- SPEC-fe67fbaa2708, proposed, will name `inputs` and no `env`.
- A later decision that ships an executor restores `env` by amending this one,
  and inherits the definition-hash argument ADR-d8a328e311b9 already made.

## Rejected

- **Implementing the three as ratified.** It builds a declaration nothing
  consumes, and a declared property nothing checks is the self-declaration
  ADR-d8a328e311b9 itself refused for `hermetic: true`.
- **Withdrawing all three.** `done` would go on rerunning the whole suite on
  every attempt, which ADR-8c12a38531e3 measured at thirteen minutes on
  Windows; the local half of the gain costs no runtime and no executor.
