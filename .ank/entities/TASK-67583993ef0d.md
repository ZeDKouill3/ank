The history, reachable. The screen before this one opens on the open work and the blockers it names, which on this corpus is a dozen rows out of five hundred; this makes the rest one key away without changing what a person sees first.

Why f and the kind cell rather than a new key: ADR-559eebf5c6f5 binds a verb's initial to the verb and gives navigation what is left, and the letters left are few (p and z are the only ones no verb begins with). f already means "narrow this listing to the next thing", and the header cell that shows the kind in force is already a touch target carved out of the header -- information made touchable. On a listing of tasks only, the next thing to narrow by is time rather than kind, and the cell says which is in force. If the human would rather keep f for kinds everywhere and spend a letter on the fold, this is the task to amend before it is claimed.

Why not the status filter of find: the graph is one connected answer, and a row filtered out of it still has to be accounted for on the rows that named it, which is what the outside count on a row is for (the CLI draws the same count in `ank graph` for blockers outside its perimeter).
