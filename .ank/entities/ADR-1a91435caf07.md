## Context

ADR-df1035934387 folds a signal kind that fires on more entities than the
smaller of 20 and five percent of the hot corpus. Five percent of a corpus
under 100 hot files is under five, and under 20 files it is nothing: a single
dead scope, a single decision ratified by its own author, arrives as "fires on
1 entity, reported once for the corpus", and the line the reader needed -- the
rename that killed the scope, with the command that repairs it (ADR-3094) -- is
only reachable through `check --kind`. A fold names five entities, so folding
five or fewer hands the reader a longer answer with less in it.

The rule also counts entities, and some signals are about something else:
`allowed_signers`, `coordination`, `origin`, a ref under `refs/ank/`, a weight
budget. Folding those produced "no-signers fires on 1 entity ...
allowed_signers".

Measured while implementing TASK-9e7a652aa4f9: with the threshold as written,
80 of the 1077 tests of ank-cli went red, and every output block of
docs/check.md turned into fold sentences. With a floor of five and entity
subjects only, 10 went red, each a contract change the task owes (the kind on
every finding, the `--kind` flag, the goldens) or a real fold (forty rewritten
entries; twelve decisions in `review`'s count).

## Decision

The threshold is never under five, and the fold counts and folds only the
signals whose subject is an entity. The rest of ADR-df1035934387 stands: one
kind per rule on every finding, a fault never folded, the five identifiers and
the command that lists the rest, and `review` and `status` counting a fold as
one. On a corpus of 400 hot files and more nothing changes.

## Consequences

- On a corpus under 100 hot files a kind is folded from its sixth entity
  instead of its first.
- A signal about the corpus, a configuration file or a ref is reported as
  before, whatever its count.

## Rejected

- **The threshold as written.** It folds one entity into a line naming that
  entity, on exactly the corpora a new reader starts with.
- **Rounding five percent up.** It moves the floor to one and leaves every
  single-entity fold below 20 files in place.
