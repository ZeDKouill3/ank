Implements ADR-a2faf59f6ce0, accepted and never coded, on top of the `inputs`
its key needs. This is the mechanism a user of ank gets for speeding up `done`
in any repository: split a suite into verifiers with disjoint `inputs`, and the
ones whose bytes did not change are reused rather than re-run.

SPEC-fe67fbaa2708 (proposed) still says `done` runs all the verifiers of the
list; it is amended here, before it is ratified, as the ADR asks.
