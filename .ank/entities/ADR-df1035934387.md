## Context

Measured on 2026-10-14 (c859d01f, 1145 hot entities): `ank check` exits 0 with
715 signals and 0 faults. By kind: 348 "written by an agent and read by no
human", 160 "verifier cargo-test changed since the proof", 113 dead scopes, 18
"ratified by its own author", 12 over-constrained scopes, 10 burst creations.

The 348 are one line per entity for a rule that, on a corpus written by
agents, fires on 30 percent of it. The 160 all come from one event, the switch
of the `cargo-test` definition to nextest (ADR-8c12a38531e3): every proof that
predates it signals, and nothing can clear them, since a proof is settled.
ADR-1e6bcbf62e61 names what that volume does: "a rule firing on a fifth of the
corpus would be the volume section 11 names as what teaches a reader to stop
reading `check`". It then applies the remedy to its own rule, "once for the
corpus and never once per mention", as ADR-3877fef1d662 does for pre-convention
authors and ADR-467ce7e9cda1 for cold entities. The remedy exists; it is
applied by hand, rule by rule, in fourteen places of `human.rs`, and the three
largest rules never received it.

`Finding` carries a level, a subject and a message, and no kind. `review`
already groups by the opening words of the message, which is a category by
accident.

## Decision

A kind on every finding, and one fold rule for every kind. Past a threshold the
per-entity lines of one kind become one corpus line that says how many, names
five, and gives the command that lists them all. The threshold is the smaller
of 20 entities and five percent of the hot corpus: on a corpus of 87 that is
four, on this one twenty.

A fault is never folded. A fault is for reading one by one, and CI exits 8 on
the first.

## Consequences

- `--json` gains `kind` on every finding, a gain within contract 1
  (ADR-6fd69efb629c).
- On this corpus, `check` would print about 30 lines where it prints 715, and
  the 18 "ratified by its own author" stay per entity, under the threshold.
- The hand-written folds of ADR-1e6bcbf62e61, ADR-3877fef1d662 and
  ADR-467ce7e9cda1 keep their text and become instances of the one rule.
- The command that lists the rest is a flag on `check` naming a kind, which the
  implementing task names; it is a flag on an existing verb and no new surface
  (ADR-9a0076dd0896).

## Rejected

- **Raising the thresholds of the rules themselves.** It silences the
  thermometer, the reasoning ADR-6e09f8a67540 gives against raising the budget.
- **A per-kind switch in config.yml.** A corpus that turns a rule off has a
  rule nobody sees; a fold keeps the count visible.
- **Folding faults.** A folded fault is a red CI with nothing to read.
